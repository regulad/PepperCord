# PepperCord privacy policy

*Effective 27 November 2026. Publisher: Parker Wahle, regulad@regulad.xyz.*

PepperCord is a Discord bot (README.md). This policy covers the two copies Parker runs, PepperCord and CordPepperer. A copy anyone else runs from this source code is theirs, and so is its policy.

## What PepperCord keeps

PepperCord keeps only what you or your server's members set up through its commands, filed under your Discord user ID or your server's ID:

- **For a server:** its command prefix; its custom commands, with their trigger and response text and how they match (a response saved without text keeps the address of the latest attachment or embedded media in the channel, which may be another member's); the categories it sorts; the webhooks it makes for `impersonate`; and its watches of Minecraft servers, Splatoon 2 and 3 rotations and sports leagues, each with its channel, role and what has been posted. A Minecraft watch also keeps the server's address and its last status, which lists the names of the players online.
- **For you:** your FNAF fazpoints, which other members can see, and the Minecraft and Splatoon watches you have sent to your direct messages.

The bot's owner can also mark a user or a server as blocked from using it. PepperCord doesn't keep your messages, your name, your nickname or your avatar.

Each item stays until the command that set it removes it (`customcommands delete`, `autosort remove`, `unwatch_minecaft_server`, `watch remove`), or, for a watch, until its channel is gone. A prefix and fazpoints have no command to clear them: to delete what PepperCord keeps about you or your server, write to regulad@regulad.xyz.

Music you play and files you `download` are saved only while they play or upload, then deleted.

## What others receive

- **YouTube, or any other site the downloader supports,** receives your search or the link you give `play`, `playtop` or `download`.
- **A Minecraft server you watch** is contacted once a minute by PepperCord's host, which learns that host's address and nothing about you.
- **ESPN, splatoon2.ink and splatoon3.ink** are asked for scores and rotations, with nothing about you.

## Diagnostics

To find and fix problems, PepperCord sends error reports and usage records to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), a service that stores them on our behalf, in the United States:

- **Errors:** where in PepperCord's code an error happened, and its message, which can repeat part of what you asked for, such as a link that failed.
- **Usage:** for one command in ten, which command ran, whether as a slash or a prefix command, how long it took and whether it worked.
- **Activity:** PepperCord's own log of starting, stopping and posting its watches, with the IDs of the channels, servers and users involved, and the address of a Minecraft server that can't be reached.

Errors and usage records carry your Discord user ID and your server's ID, so that a problem can be traced to where it happened. They don't carry what you typed into a command, your messages, your name or anything else from your account, and our diagnostics service is set not to store IP addresses.

This is used only to diagnose and improve PepperCord. It isn't sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data. Sentry keeps it for up to 90 days.

## Children

PepperCord is for Discord's users, who must be at least 13, and doesn't knowingly collect children's personal information.
